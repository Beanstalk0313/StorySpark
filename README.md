# StorySpark

## The Ultimate AI-powered story creation tool

StorySpark is your gateway to creating complex stories, fanfictions, and even participating in the story yourself.



### Some features we offer:

* Fanfictions - Creation canon-compliant fanfictions with a unique research phase and the ability to edit and save your own research files to make sure the AI actually knows what happened. No more 'good guy is a bad guy' moments!
* Original Stories - Why *only* be able to make fanfictions? With StorySpark, you can obviously make completely new stories based on your ideas.
* AI RPG - Step into your stories. With the ability to convert an existing story into an AI RPG, this function will bring hours of fun of used well.
* Research - StorySpark allows you to see and edit what will serve as the AI's base for fanfictions, so you can fine tune it to make sure it feels like the real-deal!
* Author mimicry and style - You can change what style the AI uses as well as if it should attempt to mimic the writing style of an author (🧪experimental).
* Lead the story - You can tell the AI what you want to happen in the next chapter, as well as set 'milestones', which are events you want to happen by a certain chapter.
* No lock-in - Import .pdfs, .doc(x)s, .txts, or our custom .ssbf files and bring your stories into StorySpark. We allow many different providers, including but not limited to:

  * Puter.js - uses the 'user-pays' model. We integrate it into our app, you get a free usage from them. You pay them if you need more usage.
  * Gemini API - With a generous free tier (20 requests per Gemini flash model per day; 14.4K requests to simpler Gemma models), we encourage you to use this one. We are currently working on implementing a tool that will use the Gemini API to get better fanfiction quality, so we advise you at least start your fanfictions using this one (it's free, and only needs a Google account, which you probably have!).
  * OpenAI - ChatGPT. They have a free tier, but you need to sign-up with billing information. Your choice.
  * Anthropic - Claude models. No free tier and they are quite expensive, especially Opus models. We do not recommend this one unless you're rich or something.
